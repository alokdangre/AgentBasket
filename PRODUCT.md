# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers use AgentBasket to discover products, configure purchases, review exact commerce terms,
and authorize immediate or bounded recurring purchases. Merchant staff use the same system to
operate the catalog, inventory, orders, and fulfillment without surrendering authority to an AI
model.

## Product Purpose

AgentBasket is a merchant-owned agentic commerce application. Shopping agents help customers make
choices, while deterministic services retain authority over cart mutations, checkout, schedules,
approval, and payment. Success means a customer can complete a shopping task quickly, understand
exactly what will happen, and never mistake an agent suggestion for an authorized purchase.

## Brand Personality

Warm, editorial, trustworthy. Ember should feel like a knowledgeable cafe assistant while the
interface remains precise about prices, limits, state, and authorization.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic chatbot bubbles that hide commerce state, decorative fintech dashboards, autonomous
payment language, ambiguous confirmation prompts, and interfaces that claim controls or completed
actions without a corresponding verified artifact.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next safe action visible and specific.
2. Prefer familiar controls over typed answers for bounded choices.
3. Separate configuration, draft creation, authorization, and payment as distinct states.
4. Show authoritative commerce artifacts, not model claims.
5. Preserve the merchant's warm cafe character without weakening operational clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic forms, explicit labels, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, sufficient
contrast, status text that does not rely on color alone, and reduced-motion support. Error messages
must state what remains unchanged and what the customer can do next.
